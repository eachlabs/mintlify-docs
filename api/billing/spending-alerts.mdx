Spending alerts notify your organization when charged spend over a rolling
window crosses a threshold you choose. Use them to watch active usage without
polling balances or building a separate cost monitor.

## Open Alerts

In the dashboard, open **Activity > Alerts**.

The Alerts page has two sections:

- **Rules** lists the alert thresholds configured for the current organization.
- **Recent triggers** shows the 20 most recent threshold evaluations that fired.

## Create a Rule

Select **Add rule**, choose a spend threshold and rolling window, then save the
rule. A rule can be enabled or disabled without deleting it.

Alert emails fire when the rolling charged spend exceeds the configured
threshold. For example, a `$1.00` threshold on the `Last hour` window sends an
alert when charged spend over the last hour rises above `$1.00`.

## Email Recipients

Spending alert emails are sent to the organization owner and billing managers.
Before enabling alerts for a large organization, review those roles on the team
page so the right people receive the notifications.

## Review Triggers

Recent triggers show when the alert fired, the rolling window, the observed
spend versus the threshold, delivery state, and whether the event was delivered
on time.

The email includes the threshold, charged spend, rolling window, and evaluation
time so recipients can reconcile the notification with the dashboard.
