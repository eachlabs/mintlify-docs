This page is for people using Claude in the browser, in the Claude desktop app, on a phone, or in Claude Code. Other apps that support MCP connect as the [MCP Server](/mcp#connect) page describes.

## What you get

You add the hosted each::labs MCP server at `https://mcp.eachlabs.ai/mcp` to Claude as a connector. It lets Claude use each::labs tools: the model catalog, `run_model`, the workflow tools, the video tools and the cards. The [MCP Server](/mcp#tools) page lists them. You describe the task and Claude chooses the tools.

A **card** is a small interactive panel that Claude shows inside the conversation: you upload a file on it, adjust a model's settings, pick one of several results, or choose a range on a timeline. When you press a card's button, Claude continues on its own; there is nothing to type.

Cards appear in claude.ai, Claude Desktop and the Claude mobile app. Claude Code runs in a terminal and has no cards: it shows results as text with links and asks you in the chat for files (as links), times, a crop area, settings or which variant you want. The cards are [MCP Apps](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/extensions/apps/overview) served by the each::labs MCP server.

## What you can ask

Ask in your own words. Claude chooses the model, builds its input and runs it. Each job is billed to the each::labs organization you chose at [sign-in](#first-sign-in). The cards below appear in claude.ai, Claude Desktop and the mobile app; in Claude Code, Claude asks for the same things in the chat.

### Generate images and pick one

> Give me four variants of a fantasy trading card with a fire dragon. I want to pick one.

The results arrive together on one card. Select the one you like, as often as you like; the button under them then reads **Use variant 2**, or the number you selected. Press it and Claude continues with that image. The choice is final. One request makes up to 8 variants.

### Edit a photo and compare before and after

> Add flatbread and two dip bowls to this plate, on a dark wooden table.

If you have not given the photo yet, Claude shows the [upload card](#give-a-file-as-input) first. The edited image comes back with a **Compare** button. Press it and drag the handle: the original is on the left, the result on the right.

### Turn a dozen screenshots into one slideshow

> Make a slideshow video from my 12 app screenshots, 3 seconds each.

One upload card takes the whole set. Drop all twelve at once; they upload four at a time, each with a thumbnail and a progress bar. When every file is up, Claude picks them up and returns the video on a result card with a player and a download button.

### Make music, then trim it

> Compose a 30-second upbeat lo-fi intro.

The track comes back with a player and one download button per format. Then:

> Cut it down to the part I like.

Claude shows the track on a timeline with two handles. Drag them, press **Preview** to listen, and press **Use this range**. When you give exact seconds instead, such as "keep 0:05 to 0:20", Claude trims without a card.

### Make a video from a photo

> Turn this photo into a 5-second clip where the camera slowly pushes in.

Claude asks for the photo on an upload card, runs a video model and shows the clip on a result card that plays in the conversation. A video often takes 5 to 10 minutes; Claude shows it as still running and checks again until it is done. See [When a tool says running](#when-a-tool-says-running).

### Adjust the settings before a run

> Restyle this portrait as a Monet oil painting. Let me adjust the settings first.

Claude shows the model's settings as a form: the prompt, size, number of images, seed and any other option the model takes. Change what you want and press **Run**. After the first run of a model in a conversation, Claude ends its reply with a short **Adjust generation settings** line naming a few of that model's settings; ask to change any of them and the form appears.

### Run your organization's workflow

> Which workflows do we have? Run our product clip workflow with this tote bag photo.

Claude lists the [workflows](/workflows/overview) your organization built in the dashboard. When a workflow needs inputs you have not given, Claude shows them as a form, with a file picker for each photo, video or audio input. Press **Run**; the outputs show together as a gallery.

### Cut or crop a video

> Crop this video to 9:16 for a story.

Claude shows the first frame with a rectangle. Move and resize it, or lock it to 1:1, 16:9, 9:16 or 4:5, and press **Use this crop**. To cut a clip, ask "keep the best three seconds" and use the timeline the same way as for audio. Exact numbers, such as "cut the first 10 seconds", skip the card.

## Add the connector

Pick the tab for where you use Claude. On a Team or Enterprise plan, read [Team and Enterprise plans](#team-and-enterprise-plans) first: an Owner may already have added the connector for you.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="claude.ai and Claude Desktop">
    The steps are the same in the browser and in the desktop app's chat. **Customize** is the page that holds your connectors.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Customize > Connectors">
        Go to **Customize > Connectors**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add a custom connector">
        Click **Add custom connector**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the server URL">
        Enter `https://mcp.eachlabs.ai/mcp`. Leave the OAuth client ID and secret empty; each::labs sets them up for you.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add and connect">
        Click **Add**, then **Connect**. The each::labs consent page opens; see [First sign-in](#first-sign-in).
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    On the Free plan you can add one custom connector. Connectors are saved to your account, so once you add it on the web or in the desktop app, the Claude mobile app has it too.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Claude Code">
    Add the connector from your shell:

    ```bash
    claude mcp add --transport http eachlabs https://mcp.eachlabs.ai/mcp
    ```

    Then run `/mcp` inside Claude Code, select `eachlabs`, and sign in when prompted; see [First sign-in](#first-sign-in). The tools appear once the sign-in completes.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Changes to the cards come from the each::labs server. A card always shows its current version, with no update or restart on your side.

## Team and Enterprise plans

An Owner adds the connector once for the organization, and each member then connects it and signs in with their own each::labs account.

Each member's each::labs account must belong to the each::labs organization that should pay for the runs, and the member picks that organization at sign-in. A member who signs in with a personal account and organization is billed there instead.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Owner">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open organization connectors">
        Go to **Organization settings > Connectors**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add a custom connector">
        Select **Add**, then **Custom**. If Claude asks for the connector type, choose **Web**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the server URL">
        Enter `https://mcp.eachlabs.ai/mcp`. Leave the OAuth client ID and secret empty.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add the connector">
        Click **Add**. The connector now appears under **Customize > Connectors** for members, with the **Custom** label.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Member">
    Go to **Customize > Connectors**, find the each::labs connector with the **Custom** label and select **Connect**. The each::labs consent page opens; see [First sign-in](#first-sign-in).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## First sign-in

Connecting opens the each::labs consent page in a new browser tab.

1. If you are not signed in to the each::labs dashboard, the page sends you through [sign-in](https://www.eachlabs.ai/sign-in) and back to the consent page.
2. Choose the **Organization**. The page states: *Predictions will run and bill as &lt;organization&gt;*. Every job you run from Claude is charged to this organization at the same prices as the API.
3. Review the permissions listed (the [scopes](/mcp#scopes)) and click **Approve**.

Claude stores the resulting token and refreshes it in the background; you do not sign in again. The token never appears in the conversation. To bill a different organization, disconnect the connector under **Customize > Connectors** and connect again.

If the page fails with *organization has no runner credential*, open the [dashboard](https://www.eachlabs.ai) once as that organization, then connect again.

### Tool approvals

Before running a tool that creates something (a model run, a workflow run, a file upload), Claude asks you to allow it. **Always allow** remembers the answer for that tool. Tools that only read (checking a job, listing models or workflows) run without a prompt. The first time a card appears, Claude also asks whether to show the each::labs app; allow it.

You can turn the connector off for one conversation: click the **+** button in the chat and select **Connectors**, then turn each connector on or off for that conversation.

## Give a file as input

Many requests start from your own photo, video or audio file. Tool inputs are URLs, and a file attached with Claude's paperclip stays inside Claude: it cannot reach a connector tool. When a model needs a file, Claude shows the each::labs **upload card** instead. Put the file on the card, not on the paperclip.

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | You ask for the result you want: "turn this photo into a short clip". |
| 2 | Claude shows the upload card and waits for your files. |
| 3 | Drop the files on the card, or pick them with its button, such as **Choose a photo**. On a phone the button opens the photo library. |
| 4 | The card uploads each file from your browser to each::labs storage and shows a thumbnail and progress. Nothing passes through Claude. |
| 5 | When the card has every file it asked for, it hands them to Claude by itself. If you upload fewer than it takes, press **Done, send to the chat**. |
| 6 | Claude receives the files' URLs and runs the model. |

<Frame caption="An upload card waiting for two photos">
  <img style={{ maxWidth: "440px", width: "100%" }} src="/images/mcp/01a-upload-empty.webp" alt="each::labs Upload photos card asking for the portrait and the outfit photo, with the text Drop up to 2 photos here and a Choose photos button." />
</Frame>

If Claude does not pick the upload up on its own, the card shows **Send to the chat by message**. It writes the file list into your message box; press **Send** and Claude continues. Claude shows its standard caution notice above a message an app prepared.

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| File kinds | Image, video or audio, as the model needs |
| Files per card | One card takes a whole set: up to 50 files, or fewer when the chosen model accepts fewer. A card for one specific file, such as the face to use, takes exactly one |
| File size | 100 MB per file |
| Upload | Four files upload at a time; the rest wait their turn. Choosing more files than the card takes, or a file over 100 MB, is refused before anything uploads |
| Time to pick | Claude waits for the card up to 60 seconds at a time. After each wait it reads any new message from you, then waits again. If it stops waiting before you are done, tell Claude when the upload is finished |
| Storage | The file is kept for 180 days, or for your organization's own retention period, as with [Upload file](/storage/upload-file). Anyone who has the file's link can open it; links are long random addresses that are not listed anywhere |
| Already have a URL | Paste a public URL in the message instead; no card is needed |
| Claude Code | Does not show cards. Claude asks in the chat for a public link to the file instead |

## Cards in the conversation

Besides the upload card, these cards appear. [What you can ask](#what-you-can-ask) shows each one.

| You see | When |
| --- | --- |
| The result: the image, a video or audio player, and download buttons | Every run that produces media. A file in several formats has one button per format. A workflow's outputs show together as a gallery |
| Several results to choose from | You asked for several variants of one prompt, up to 8 in one request. Select one as often as you like, then press the button under them, which reads **Use variant** and the number you selected. Claude continues with that one, and the choice is final |
| A form with the model's settings and a **Run** button | You ask to adjust settings, or a run has options worth choosing first |
| A **Compare** button under an edited image or video | The result is an edit of a file you gave. Drag the handle to compare before and after |
| A timeline with two handles, or a rectangle on the first frame | You want to cut a clip or crop a frame and have not given exact numbers. Press **Use this range** or **Use this crop**. Trimmed audio keeps its format; FLAC comes back as mp4 |
| A workflow's inputs as a form, with a file picker for each photo, video or audio input | A workflow needs inputs you have not given |

A card that cannot load in your app says so and tells you what to give in the chat instead, such as a public file link or the start and end times.

A card you already used stays locked on your choice when you come back to the conversation later. To see a result again, ask Claude to show it; it shows the file on a card without running anything.

## When a tool says running

A tool waits about 20 seconds for its job. A job that takes longer, such as a long video or a multi-step workflow, shows as still running, and Claude checks again until the job is done, then shows the result. The job keeps running on each::labs the whole time. You do not need to ask; if Claude stops before the job is done, ask "is that job done?" and it checks again.

When you asked for several variants, Claude checks on all of them together, and they arrive on one card with the picker once every variant is done.

Claude can also look up your organization's recent runs, for example to pick up a run it started earlier after a brief connection interruption, and continue with it rather than starting a new one. That list shows only each run's id, model, status, time and result link.

Long videos can take 5 to 10 minutes. Claude keeps checking until yours is ready, and asks you before trying a different model, so you are never charged for a run you did not ask for.

You are billed once, when the job runs, not for checking. Details are under [Long jobs](/mcp#long-jobs).

Results are URLs to files on each::labs storage, kept as long as [uploads](#give-a-file-as-input). Download anything you want to keep longer. A data result, such as a transcript or an analysis, comes back as text without a card.
