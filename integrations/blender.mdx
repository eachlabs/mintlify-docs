The each::labs extension sends what you already make in Blender to each::labs models and workflows, then brings the results back into your file:

- **Generate Image from View** renders the current frame, or snapshots the viewport, and sends it with your prompt. The result opens in the Image Editor and can sit behind the camera for comparison.
- **Generate Video from Camera Move** renders your frame range as a clip, sends it with your prompt, and adds the generated video, with its sound, to the Video Sequencer at the right frame.
- **Re-run** sends the last rendered inputs again with another model, prompt or setting, without rendering.
- **Export movie** renders the sequencer, picture and sound, to one MP4.

Everything lives in an **Eachlabs** tab in the sidebar of the 3D Viewport and the Video Sequencer.

## Requirements

- Blender 4.2 or later.
- An each::labs account with credits, and an API key. See [Authentication](/authentication) to create one.
- Online access allowed in Blender. Blender asks the first time it starts; you can change it later in **Edit > Preferences > System > Network > Allow Online Access**. Without it, the Eachlabs tab tells you what to enable and makes no requests.

## Install the extension

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From the each::labs repository">
    Adding the repository once lets Blender install the extension and update it along with your other extensions.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Add the repository">
        Open **Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions**, open the **Repositories** menu, press **+** and choose **Add Remote Repository**. Paste this URL and press **OK**:

        ```
        https://github.com/eachlabs/blender-extension/releases/latest/download/index.json
        ```
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install">
        Search for **Eachlabs** and press **Install**. The listing shows the two permissions the extension asks for: **Network**, to send renders and download results, and **Files**, to save them next to your `.blend` file.

        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/blender/get-extensions.png" alt="Get Extensions listing the Eachlabs extension with its Install button and permissions" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From a downloaded file">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Download the extension">
        Download the latest `eachlabs-<version>.zip` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/eachlabs/blender-extension/releases/latest). Keep it zipped.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install it">
        Drag the zip into a Blender window and confirm, or open **Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions**, open the menu in the top-right corner and choose **Install from Disk**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    A file install does not update itself. Install a newer zip the same way to upgrade.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Connect your API key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste the key">
    Open **Edit > Preferences > Add-ons**, expand **Eachlabs** and paste your key into **API key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Press **Test connection**. A working key shows your balance; a rejected one shows the error the API returned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blender/preferences.png" alt="The Eachlabs add-on preferences with the API key field, Test connection and the default video model" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Blender stores add-on preferences, including this key, unencrypted in its `userpref.blend` file. To keep the key out of Blender, leave the field empty and set the `EACHLABS_API_KEY` environment variable before you start Blender.
</Warning>

The same page sets the **Default video model**, its inputs and its parameters. Change it if you prefer another model for video.

## Open the Eachlabs tab

In the 3D Viewport or the Video Sequencer, press `N` to open the sidebar and select the **Eachlabs** tab. Press the refresh button next to **Models not loaded** once per session. It loads the model list and, with a key, your workflows.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blender/first-open.png" alt="The Eachlabs tab before an API key is set, with Set API key and the refresh button" width="440" />
</Frame>

The tab has four panels:

| Panel | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Eachlabs** | Shows whether online access and your key are set, and how many models and workflows are loaded. |
| **Generate Image from View** | Sends one frame to an image model or workflow. |
| **Generate Video from Camera Move** | Sends a frame range to a video model or workflow, and holds **Export movie**. |
| **Jobs** | Lists every run with its state, elapsed time and settled cost. |

<Tip>
  Save your `.blend` file before you generate. Inputs and results are then kept in an `eachlabs/<file name>/` folder next to it; an unsaved file uses a temporary folder.
</Tip>

## Generate an image from your view

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blender/image-panel.png" alt="Generate Image from View with Nano Banana Pro selected, its listed price, a prompt and Rendered still bound to image_urls" width="440" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a model">
    Type in the search field under **Generate Image from View**. Only models and workflows that take an image and return an image are listed; tick **All URL-input models** to see more. The model's listed price appears under it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the prompt">
    Describe the result you want. If the model takes a seed, tick **Seed** to fix it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check where your frame goes">
    Under **Send render to**, the model input that receives your frame should read **Rendered still**. It is set for you when the model has one image input. Use **File…** to send another image, such as a style reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Choose **Render** to use your render engine at scene resolution, or **Viewport** for a fast snapshot of the view, then press **Generate Image**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The job appears under **Jobs**. When it finishes, the result opens in an Image Editor. With **Camera background** on, it also shows behind the camera: look through the camera with `Numpad 0` and set its opacity in the camera's **Background Images**.

## Generate a video from a camera move

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blender/video-panel.png" alt="Generate Video from Camera Move with Seedance 2.5 reference-to-video, the clip bound to video_urls, draft and 480p ticked" width="440" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Animate your camera">
    Keyframe the camera over the range you want. The scene range is used unless you untick **Scene range** and set your own start and end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the model">
    Once the model list loads, the default video model is selected: **Seedance 2.5 reference-to-video**, with your camera move as its reference video, at 480p in draft mode. Draft gives a faster, cheaper preview. The ★ button restores the default after you try other models.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the prompt">
    Seedance refers to its inputs by token: `@Video1` is your camera move and `@Image1` the first image you send. For example:

    ```
    Follow the camera movement of @Video1. A bronze statue on a stone plinth in a museum courtyard at golden hour. Sound: quiet courtyard ambience and distant birds; no music, no dialogue.
    ```

    Seedance also generates sound, so describe it, for example *Sound: quiet courtyard ambience; no music, no dialogue.* A prompt without a sound direction can be refused when the generated audio resembles copyrighted music.

    Avoid the words *add*, *remove*, *replace*, *extend* and *continue*. They switch Seedance to video editing, which ignores the requested length.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bind your inputs">
    Under **Send render to**, choose what fills each model input: **Clip (MP4)**, **First frame**, **Last frame**, or **File…** for anything else, such as a character sheet in `image_urls` or music in `audio_urls`. For models with separate first- and last-frame inputs, set each one yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the timing">
    **Preview** sets the resolution of the clip you send. **Match duration** asks the model for the length you rendered, so the result fits your range. If it still differs, **Timing** decides: **Fit to range** stretches the result to your frames, **Keep timing** plays it in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Press **Generate Video**. Blender renders the range, and the result lands on the first free sequencer channel at your start frame, with its sound on the next channel when **Import audio** is on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blender/video-result.png" alt="The generated clip on the Video Sequencer timeline, with the Eachlabs tab in the sequencer's sidebar" />
</Frame>

The Eachlabs tab is also in the Video Sequencer's sidebar, so you can generate, re-run and follow jobs while you edit.

**Parameters** lists the model's other options, such as resolution, duration and aspect ratio. Tick an option to send your value; unticked options use the model's defaults. **JSON** takes extra inputs the list cannot show, such as several reference images:

```json
{"image_urls": ["https://example.com/character.png", "https://example.com/style.png"]}
```

## Follow, re-run and cancel jobs

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blender/jobs.png" alt="The Jobs panel with a finished video and image job, their run time and settled cost" width="440" />
</Frame>

- **Jobs** shows each run's state and elapsed time, and its settled cost once it finishes. The ▶ button opens a finished result in your system player or viewer, and **Open folder** shows the files.
- The ↺ button next to **Generate Image** and **Generate Video** sends the last rendered inputs again with whatever is now selected: another model, prompt, seed or parameter. Nothing is rendered again, and uploaded files are reused for up to six days.
- The **×** button stops a running job. Work the provider has already started may still finish and be billed. Workflow runs cannot be cancelled yet, so cancelling only stops waiting for them.
- **Export movie** renders everything in the sequencer, with sound, to `eachlabs/<file name>/<id>_movie.mp4` and lists it under **Jobs**.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The tab says online access is disabled">
    Enable **Edit > Preferences > System > Network > Allow Online Access**, or start Blender without `--offline-mode`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invalid or missing API key (HTTP 401)">
    Paste the key again in the add-on preferences and press **Test connection**. If you use `EACHLABS_API_KEY`, leave the field empty, or the field wins.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A model is missing from the list">
    Press refresh in the Eachlabs panel, then tick **All URL-input models**. The default list shows only models whose inputs and outputs match the action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A job failed with a provider message">
    The message under the job comes from the model provider, for example a content or copyright refusal. Change the prompt or inputs and press ↺.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The video was stretched and its sound is muted">
    With **Fit to range**, a result that is not the rendered length is stretched to fit, and its sound is added muted because Blender cannot stretch sound strips. Unmute it, or use **Keep timing**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Limitations

- Uploads are limited to 100 MB per file. Lower **Preview** or shorten the range for long shots.
- Workflows are listed only when their inputs declare image or video types, and they have no listed price.
- The panel shows each model's listed price, not a quote for your exact settings; the settled cost appears on the job.
- Seedance drafts are not finished at full resolution from Blender yet.
